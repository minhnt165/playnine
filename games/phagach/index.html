How to play

Bounce the ball with the paddle at the bottom and smash every brick to clear the level. If the ball falls past the bottom you lose a life; you start with 3 lives.

Controls

  • Phone: drag sideways anywhere on the board and the paddle follows the drag (no need to touch the paddle itself). Tap to launch the ball.
  • Computer: move the mouse, or ←→ / AD. Space or click to launch, P / Esc to pause.
  • A ball that hits the middle of the paddle goes straight up; near the edge it bounces off at an angle. Use the edges to steer.

Bricks

  • Normal brick: breaks in 1 hit.
  • Tough brick: takes 2 or 3 hits, cracking a little more each time.
  • Steel brick: unbreakable, and not needed to clear the level.

Power-ups

Some bricks drop a power-up when they break. Catch it with the paddle; the time left shows at the top right.

  • Wide paddle: a longer paddle for 15 seconds.
  • Multi-ball: every ball splits into three. As long as one ball is still in play, you keep your life.
  • Slow ball: the ball slows down for 12 seconds.
  • Fireball: for 8 seconds the ball smashes any brick at once (tough ones too) and passes through, bouncing only off steel.
  • Extra life: +1 life (max 5).

Scoring

  • Normal brick 10 points, tough brick 30 / 50 points, catching a power-up 25 points. Clearing a level earns 100 × the level number.
  • Combo: keep breaking bricks before the ball comes back to the paddle and points are multiplied x2 from the 5th brick, x3 from the 9th... up to x5.
  • Clear all 10 levels to beat the game; play then loops back to level 1 with a faster ball.
