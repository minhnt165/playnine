How to play

Shoot bubbles from the bottom. When a bubble touches the ceiling or another bubble it sticks. 3 or more connected bubbles of the same colour pop, and every bubble no longer connected to the ceiling drops for bonus points.

Controls

  • Phone: drag your finger above the shooter to aim, release to shoot.
  • Computer: point with the mouse and click to shoot.
  • Bubbles bounce off the side walls.

Pressure

  • Every few misses a new row pushes the board down.
  • If any bubble reaches the dashed line near the shooter, the game is over.
  • You only get colours that are still on the board.

Scoring

  • 10 points per popped bubble.
  • Dropped bubbles are worth 20, 40, then 60 points each.
  • Clear the board for a bonus.
